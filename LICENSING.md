# Licensing

Sublime is free software under the GNU Affero General Public License,
version 3 or later (`LICENSE`). In plain words:

- Use it for anything: personal, hobby, research, open source, at work,
  commercially. Copy it, change it, share it.
- If you build on it and distribute the result, or run a modified version
  as a service other people use, share your source under the same terms.
  That is the whole ask. The point is that improvements flow back to
  everyone, and nobody puts a paywall around this work without giving back.

Using the `sublime` binary as a separate program from your own code, open or
closed, is not building on it. Only modifying Sublime or embedding its code
carries the share-alike obligation.

## Contributions

## If the AGPL does not fit

If you have a situation where the share-alike terms genuinely do not work,
open a discussion on the repository and say what you are building. Every
case is handled on its own.
